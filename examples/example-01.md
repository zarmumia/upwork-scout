# Examples (real screening decisions, client details removed)

## 1. Skipped — the client wants someone else
**Job:** AI Automation & Business Systems Lead. Discovery with department heads, automations, SOPs, training.
**Fit:** very close to the work she does.
**Why skipped:** client's preferred location excluded her; account opened that day, payment not verified; rate cap far below the level asked.
**Lesson:** a great description doesn't outweigh three warning signs together.

## 2. Skipped — too crowded
**Job:** AI video editing automation, one-time project. Strong client (high rating, tens of thousands spent).
**Why skipped:** 20–50 proposals, 9 already interviewing, 17 Connects.
**Lesson:** a strong client with a long line is still a long line.

## 3. Skipped — red flag
**Job:** a full Squarespace website for a small studio, $5.
**Client:** 5.0 rating, 100+ jobs posted, 90+ hires, under $1,000 total spent, open jobs in every field.
**Why skipped:** rating-farming / free-labor pattern. No flexibility on red flags.

## 4. Skipped — not interesting
**Job:** one call to switch a Facebook profile to a page. Excellent client (5.0, dozens of reviews, high spend).
**Why skipped:** the client was fine; the work wasn't substantive.
**Lesson:** a good client doesn't make a job worth doing.

## 5. Shown with a flag — strong fit, weak client
**Job:** Hebrew product/growth review of a SaaS for the Israeli market: paywall, pricing, trust signals, RTL.
**Fit:** exactly her outside-in review work, plus payments background.
**Flag:** client had no reviews and no spend.
**Her decision:** skipped ("too risky"). The agent was right to show it; she was right to decide.
